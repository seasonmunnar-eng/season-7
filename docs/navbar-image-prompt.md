Quiet understated premium nature-resort mood. Wide 3:1 composition, fine natural botanical texture across frame, dark low-contrast centre for cream navigation text, leaves reaching in from both sides. No lettering, no monogram, no logo, no typography, no border, no UI, no watermark. This must remain attractive when cropped to a very shallow horizontal navigation strip.
